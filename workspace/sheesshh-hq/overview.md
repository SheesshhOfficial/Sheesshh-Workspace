# Sheesshh HQ

Your home for projects, notes and agent-assisted work.

## Start here

1. Follow the repository's `docs/sheesshh-setup.md` to build the desktop app.
2. Open Chats and choose a CLI you have installed and signed into.
3. Create a project folder and ask the agent to register a Workspace card.
4. Keep reusable lessons in `knowledge/` and workflows in `processes/`.

## Your project space

Add your active projects here as you choose them. No personal details or external
accounts are assumed. The other four starter cards are examples from the upstream
workspace; archive them when you no longer need them.

Repository: [SheesshhOfficial/Sheesshh-Workspace](https://github.com/SheesshhOfficial/Sheesshh-Workspace)
