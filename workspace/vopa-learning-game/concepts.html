Review notes across the set

  • Number-line, bird-count, shape, picture-word and addition examples have been visually checked for the pictured answers. This is concept review, not automated gameplay/content testing.
  • The dense illustrated detail and serif display type need a later simplification, accessibility and phone-readability pass.
  • Character consistency is not locked: the completion screen’s goggles and some mail-prop continuity require review.
  • Future text, buttons and controls should be real runtime UI, not cropped from these images. Every house and its animated customer must be separate production assets; the flattened route concept is not the final asset structure.
  • Home/settings concepts and separate animation-art assets remain unproduced after the generation limit stopped the batch at twelve. No animations or rigs were generated.
