What the learner will do

Play resumes the earliest unfinished curriculum stage, not the last game visited. Explore opens the six-fort route. Fort 1 contains eight sequential named games, but this demo implements only MathFly and NumberLineJump. Each attempt has three lives; a wrong answer or a ten-second question timeout loses one life and advances to a new question.

English is the base language with spoken explanation narration now. Text and audio will use stable keys so Marathi can be added through settings later. Age group and syllabus are not confirmed; the old COD Class 1 scope must not be silently carried over.
