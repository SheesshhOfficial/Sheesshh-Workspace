Play and progression

Home Play selects the earliest unfinished curriculum stage. Explore shows six sequential forts. Fort 1 contains eight sequential game slots; the demo scope is MathFly and NumberLineJump, with other planned content locked.

  • Four stages per implemented game; three cumulative correct answers complete a stage.
  • Three lives per attempt; a wrong answer or ten-second timeout loses one life and advances to a new question.
  • One star per stage and one sticker per completed game, without duplicate replay rewards.
  • The daily 40-minute limit is currently OFF.

Six forts × eight games is a future 48-game plan, not delivered content.
