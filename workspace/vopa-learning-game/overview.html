The confirmed game loop

  1. Choose a themed city
  2. Tap the next house
  3. Watch the cat travel automatically in a tiny airship
  4. Meet an animal customer
  5. Complete a short set of learning questions
  6. Deliver the letter and celebrate with an envelope popup and hearts
  7. Animate travel onward to the next house; completed houses remain available for practice

Cloud City is the first city, built from houses on floating islands. English questions and customer introductions use voice narration, matching text and a replay button. Gentle hints and retries replace life penalties. Replaying completed houses uses fresh practice while preserving their delivered status. Every house and every house’s animated animal customer must be separate assets. Exact onward-travel timing relative to the delivery popup or Continue remains open.

Visual direction

Rich layered 2D, not a static illustration and not full 3D. The portrait route scrolls vertically. The eventual game should feel alive through useful character, airship and environmental motion while keeping questions readable and birds countable.

The twelve images explore that direction. Their text, buttons, character placements and proposed route are concept art, not final UI specifications or engine-ready scenes. Future text and buttons should be rendered at runtime, not baked into the background.

What is ready and what is not

  • Ready to review: twelve generated visual concepts, ten uploaded asset drafts with per-file checks, confirmed design decisions, proposed house mapping, and the detailed asset/animation checklist.
  • Not generated: separate home/settings concepts and the originally requested generated animation-art batch. The ten later user uploads are reviewed separately; their presence does not establish production readiness. The image-generation limit stopped the batch before these were produced.
  • Not implemented: rigs, sprite frames, animation clips, scene assembly, navigation, question logic, narration playback, saved progress or a playable CCD build.
  • Not tested: Unity integration, animation loops, actual phone layout/performance and classroom use.
