Cat On Delivery (COD)

A playable Unity learning-game prototype with editable screens: a cat courier delivers letters through Cloud City, one learning activity at a time.

What works now

Number Line, Count Birds, Shape Match, Picture–Word Match and Add/Subtract run on a fixed route. The tiny airship travels automatically, wrong answers get gentle hints and retries, and completed deliveries show envelopes and hearts. Replaying a house keeps its completion without awarding duplicate delivery rewards.
