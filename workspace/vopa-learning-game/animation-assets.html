Animation & Asset Plan

Make CCD feel alive while keeping the learning task readable. This is a production checklist, not a claim that animated or rig-ready assets already exist.

Current status: twelve complete concept images exist. Separate transparent assets, cut-out rig parts, bird sprite frames, animation clips and rigs have not been generated. The generation limit blocked the remaining home/settings concepts and asset batch; no fallback generation was used.

Asset-by-asset checklist

Confirmed: every house is an individual asset and every house’s animal customer is an individual animated asset. After each completion the cat/mail courier moves onward to the next house with animation. Exact popup/continue timing is still open. Recording this note does not authorize game implementation.

Courier cat

  • Approved neutral full-body design and consistent scale.
  • Separate head, torso, upper/lower arms, hands/paws, legs/feet, tail sections, ears where needed, hat, scarf and mailbag.
  • Complete hidden areas beneath overlaps; transparent padding and clean alpha edges.
  • Expression set: neutral/happy, think, encouraging retry, delighted delivery; eye/blink and mouth variants.
  • Defined pivots, layering order, bone limits and attachment points for props/airship.

Five customer drafts

Proposed rabbit, squirrel, hedgehog, owl and bear. Each needs a consistent scale sheet, neutral pose, appropriate separated parts, blinks, greeting and celebration. Do not assume a still character in a concept image is complete behind the occluded edges.

Tiny airship

Separate balloon panels/body, basket/hull, ropes/struts, propeller, fins, flag, mail storage and courier attachment. Define the travel pivot and docking position. Verify no seams or disconnections during bob, turn or landing.

Bird loop

Prepare coherent flap frames with consistent body volume, colour, canvas size and registration. Define a resting/settled state and arrival path. Review loop seam, timing, silhouettes and countability. No frames or loop are ready yet.

Environment layers

Sky gradient, far clouds, middle cloud banks, distant islands, five independent house assets and their island/base layers, foreground vegetation, docking platforms and small ambient props. Do not bake all houses and customers into the map background. Provide edge coverage for parallax, transparent layers, depth order and safe zones behind question UI.

UI and effects

Button backgrounds/states without baked labels, envelope icon/opening pieces, heart particles, route markers, lock/completed badges, audio/replay icons and hint state. Real text, scalable controls and input states belong to the runtime UI. Home/settings visual concepts are still pending.

Audio and content

English question/customer scripts, reviewed recordings, replay timing, gentle feedback and delivery sounds. Match each clip to stable content IDs; retain text equivalents, mute controls and an interruption policy.

Suggested animation state list

  • Courier: idle, greet, think, encouraging retry, hold mail, hand over mail, celebrate, travel idle.
  • Customers: idle/blink, greet, listen, receive, happy response.
  • Airship: parked idle, depart, travel, arrive/dock. Automatic travel to the selected house; animated onward travel after each completed house. Popup/continue timing still needs definition.
  • Birds: arrive, settle, small readable idle, depart after the question is resolved.
  • UI: selection feedback, hint reveal, correct answer, delivery popup, next-house unlock.

The exact clip list and timings need review. These are not existing clips. Avoid conveying incorrect answers with frightening reactions or a life penalty.

Required asset and loop QA

  1. Inspect true transparency, no baked checkerboards, halos or background fragments.
  2. Check complete part coverage, safe overlaps, coherent proportions and no missing limbs behind crops.
  3. Validate canvas scale, pivots, registration, file names and consistent import settings.
  4. Test rig deformation through intended motion ranges, keeping expressions and props aligned.
  5. Check frame continuity, duplicate/missing frames and loop seams at actual playback speed.
  6. Measure target-device performance and avoid excessive particle/texture cost.
  7. Verify visual states match game/audio state; interrupted actions recover cleanly.
  8. Test reduced motion, readable focus, calm question scenes and countable settled birds.
