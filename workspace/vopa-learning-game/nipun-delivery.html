Acceptance · navigation and rewards

  • Play always finds the earliest unfinished curriculum stage even after exploring a later completed game.
  • Six forts appear in order; Fort 1 alone initially unlocks. Its eight named slots are visible, with only the two demo games eligible through sequential progression.
  • Stars and stickers persist once earned; replay never duplicates them.
  • No fort flag or next-fort completion reward is granted after only the two demo games.

Acceptance · question loop

  • Each game has four sequential stages; three cumulative correct answers complete a stage.
  • Three lives reset per attempt. Wrong answer and ten-second timeout each lose one life and advance to a new question.
  • The timer starts only after the relevant narration finishes; instructions pause it and resume safely.
  • Double taps, timeout/answer races and repeated popup closure cannot double-charge a life or duplicate completion.
  • Retry resets only the current stage attempt; earlier completed progress remains.
  • MathFly button-driven bird motion has no collision penalty; day/night is selected per attempt.

Acceptance · daily time and presentation

  • Persist a local 40-minute window beginning at first daily open; leaving the page or opening instructions does not pause it.
  • At expiry finish the active question, then block another; reset at midnight without silently granting extra same-day time.
  • Keep layout edits saved outside Play; test Home/Explore/game transitions without resetting designer placement.
  • Verify readable portrait layout, real touch/mouse input and English narration in the actual browser build.
  • Test reload, interrupted narration and local save recovery; do not claim backend or cross-device enforcement.
