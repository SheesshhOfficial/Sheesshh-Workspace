1. World and first scope

  • Confirmed: a map of multiple themed cities, each with houses and customers waiting for mail. Class/difficulty is selected separately from city theme.
  • Confirmed: first city is Cloud City, with five houses on floating islands.
  • Confirmed: first detailed slice is Class 1; the overall intended audience is Classes 1–5.
  • Confirmed: mixed animal customers, a cat courier and a tiny airship.
  • Proposed cast: kitten courier with rabbit, squirrel, hedgehog, owl and bear customers. These exact character assignments are visual draft choices, not separately approved.

The themed cities visible on the world-map concept are examples of future expansion, not completed cities or a fixed production commitment.

2. End-to-end house workflow

  1. Select: tap the next available house on the fixed route.
  2. Travel: the airship carries the cat there automatically. No manual flight controls are confirmed.
  3. Arrive: the customer greets the cat; English narration, text and replay explain the activity.
  4. Learn: answer a short set of three to five questions using that house’s activity. Three questions is the proposed starting count shown in the concepts, not a separately confirmed exact rule.
  5. Try again: a mistake leads to a gentle hint and retry. No loss of lives; hearts are celebration, not health.
  6. Deliver: after the set is complete, hand over the mail and show the envelope popup with hearts.
  7. Continue: preserve completion, unlock the next house and return to the route or continue there.
  8. Revisit: a completed house offers fresh questions without erasing its delivered status.

Question formats with selectable options were explicitly requested. They are part of CCD’s direction; earlier brainstorming preferences against quiz-style mechanics do not override that choice.

4. Progression and replay

Confirmed: a fixed sequential route; completing one house unlocks the next. Completed houses remain available for fresh practice and keep their completed-delivery marker. The first city has five houses.

Still to specify: question-bank size, repeat avoidance, progress-save format, restart/resume behaviour, language-independent content IDs, and whether a partial question set resumes or restarts. These are design tasks, not working systems.

Class selection should reflect reviewed content rather than claim that a city proves mastery. Exact Classes 2–5 progression is outside the first Class 1 slice and needs a separate curriculum/content pass.

5. Screens, visuals and audio

Portrait phone view with a vertically scrolling city route. Layered illustrated 2D must be lively, not just one still picture. Proposed motion includes parallax, drifting clouds, airship bobbing/propeller, cat reactions, customer blinks/waves, house ambience and envelope/heart celebration. Individual motion choices remain proposals until the animation plan is reviewed.

Confirmed English support: questions and customer introductions have narration, matching visible text and a replay control. Actual recordings, timing, button behaviour and accessibility are not built. Keep movement calmer during questions; narration must never be the only way to understand the task.

Concept UI is a visual guide. Future implementation must create real text, buttons, focus states, hit areas, audio controls and progress indicators. Baked illustration lettering is not a localization or accessibility solution.

6. Planning-to-production workflow

  1. Review the twelve concept images and resolve remaining route/content choices.
  2. Prepare separate transparent art assets and approve consistent character/environment designs.
  3. Specify and test rigs, frame loops, component motion and state transitions.
  4. Only after implementation authorization, assemble one house as a vertical slice.
  5. Verify question correctness, audio/text agreement, retry, delivery, replay, saving and phone usability.
  6. Expand to the other four houses after the first house works. Later cities and Classes 2–5 follow separately reviewed scope.

There is no playable CCD build, animation system or native test result at this stage.

7. Decisions still open

  • Approve the proposed five-house mapping/order and exact animal assignments.
  • Choose the initial exact question count within the accepted three-to-five range.
  • Define Class 1 ranges, vocabulary, answer distractors and content-review criteria.
  • Review the final courier/cast design and asset breakdown.
  • Specify home/settings and save/resume behaviour.
  • Choose technical rig/animation tooling after a feasibility sample, not from the concept pictures alone.
